Roster

2026–27 D1 rosters. Numbers are 2026 Road to Nationals averages and NQS (final regular-season RQS) per event. AA = sum of her 2026 event NQS (season average where an event has no NQS) over the events she did in 2026, e.g. UB+BB 19.810. It's a derived number, not an RTN AA score; RTN's own AA NQS is shown next to it where one exists. They guide the draft; they don't score anything. NEW = no 2026 NCAA record (freshman or newcomer). no RQS = competed in 2026 but not enough on that event for an RQS. no 2026 scores = on a 2026 roster, never competed. None of these get a projection.
