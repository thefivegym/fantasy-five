"Submit with GitHub" opens a pre-filled issue on thefivegym/fantasy-five. Sign in to GitHub if asked, then press Create / Submit new issue. A bot validates it within a minute or two, saves it, comments, and closes the issue. Your GitHub login is your player name. Resubmitting before the lock replaces your lineup. Issues are public, so your picks are visible once submitted.
