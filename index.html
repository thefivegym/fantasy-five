How it works

  1. Sign up once with your public Instagram handle. No GitHub account for sign-up. The handle is public on the leaderboard; it's how we contact weekly winners. Submitting it does not verify that you own the account.
  2. Five picks a week: one Vault, one Bars, one Beam, one Floor, one All-Around. Anywhere in D1.
  3. Event picks score her real score on that event. All-Around: any gymnast. Her score is every event she competes that meet, added up.
  4. Five different teams, and a gymnast fills one slot. Team total is the five scores added up: about 80 with an all-arounder in the AA slot, less with a specialist.
  5. Competes twice: best score (or best AA meet) counts; AA never mixes meets. Doesn't compete that slot: 0.
  6. New lineup every week. Nothing carries over; no lineup, no score.
  7. Lineups lock Friday 5:00 PM CT, before the week's first meet. Submit through a GitHub issue; your login is your name on the board.
  8. Highest weekly total wins. Tied for the top score: the $25 gift card is split evenly between the tied players (e.g. two-way tie = $12.50 each), and every tied player gets the story shoutout. The season leaderboard adds up weekly totals for bragging rights only.
  9. A Wednesday reminder is a GitHub mention, only for players who signed up with a GitHub account. A handle-only signup is not mentioned and is not emailed.

Weekly prize: Weekly winner gets a $25 digital Starbucks gift card and a story shoutout on @thefivethatscore. Highest weekly total wins.
