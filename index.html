How it works

  1. Pick 5 D1 gymnasts on each event: vault, bars, beam, floor. 20 slots. Same gymnast can fill more than one event.
  2. Each slot scores her real score on that event that week. Competes twice: best score counts. Doesn't compete: 0.
  3. Team total is all 20 slots added up. No drops.
  4. Lineups lock Friday 5:00 PM CT, before the week's first meet. Resubmit as often as you like until then.
  5. You submit through a GitHub issue under your own account. Your GitHub login is your name on the board.
